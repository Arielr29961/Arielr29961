## About me

I turn messy business data into metrics, models, and decisions that leadership actually acts on.

- **Business Analyst at Mimun Yashir** (consumer finance) — built a company-wide debt-collection-rate KPI for legal portfolios from scratch: SQL Server data models, pipelines, driver analysis and forecasting, presented to the CEO and executive team.
- **M.Sc. in Machine Learning & Data Science** — Reichman University (Efi Arazi School of Computer Science)
- **B.A. in Philosophy, Politics & Economics (PPE)** — Hebrew University of Jerusalem
- Previously: Data Analyst at the **Israeli Ministry of Justice** (AML data, Financial Intelligence Unit) and Economist at the **Ministry of Communications** (national fiber-optic tender, Power BI analytics platform).
- **Looking for:** Product / Data Analyst roles in high-tech, on the path to Data Science.
